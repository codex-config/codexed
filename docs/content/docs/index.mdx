title: Introduction
description: What Codexed is and how it works.
---

Codexed helps you manage the configuration of the Codex CLI. You can edit `config.toml`, `AGENTS.md`, skills, MCP servers and feature flags for all your projects at once, or for one folder. You can also install plugins and add marketplaces.

## How it works

Codexed is a single Go binary.

- When it starts, it adds an icon to the system tray. On macOS, the icon is in the menu bar.
- The tray menu has three items: **Open**, **GitHub** and **Quit**.
- **Open** shows the interface in your browser at `http://127.0.0.1:7378`.
- The server only listens on localhost. Other computers on your network cannot reach it.

Codexed keeps running in the tray until you quit it. You can close the browser tab at any time and open it again from the tray.

Codexed works through the Codex CLI on your computer. It asks Codex which models, skills, plugins and feature flags exist, and Codex makes every change to `config.toml`. So you need the [Codex CLI](/docs/installation#the-codex-cli) too.

## What you can manage

- Global configuration and instructions, in `~/.codex`. You change settings with a form or as TOML.
- Project configuration and instructions, in any project folder.
- Skills. You can turn them on or off, edit your own, and write new ones.
- MCP servers, globally or for one project.
- Feature flags, globally or for one project.
- Plugins, which you install from marketplaces.
- The plugin marketplaces that Codex knows about.

## Find your way around

The sidebar has two parts:

- At the top, your projects.
- At the bottom, the sections: **Configuration**, **Instructions**, **Skills**, **Plugins**, **Marketplaces**, **MCP Servers** and **Features**.

The **⋯** menu next to the filter adds a project, and it switches between two views of your projects:

- **Tree view** shows your projects inside their folders. Select the arrow next to a folder to open or close it. Only folders that hold projects are in the tree.
- **List view** shows the most recently used projects first.

To hide the sidebar, choose the codexed logo. The logo stays in the corner. Choose it again to bring the sidebar back. **⌘B** (or **Ctrl+B**) does the same.

With no project selected, each section shows your global configuration. Select a project to see the same section for that project. To go back to the global view, select the project again, or choose **×** next to its name at the top of the page.

The **ⓘ** button at the top right shows the versions of Codexed and Codex.
